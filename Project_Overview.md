RoomCraft AR — Project Overview & Vision

1. Executive Summary

RoomCraft AR is a web-based augmented reality experience that allows anyone to customize their physical living space by summoning 3D virtual objects directly into their room. Whether it is an aesthetic piece of furniture, an animated collectible figure, or an ambient interactive lamp, users can curate, place, and arrange items they have always wanted in their personal space.

The application runs directly inside modern mobile web browsers with zero installation required, remembers where objects were placed across multiple visits, and visually blends virtual objects into the real environment through realistic lighting and contact shadows.

2. The Core Problem & Opportunity

Living Space Limitations: Many people want to personalize their rooms, desks, or workspaces with items they admire (art, rare collectibles, high-end decor, ambient lighting), but are constrained by budget, physical space, or availability.

App Friction: Traditional AR apps require downloading large native applications from app stores, creating a high barrier to entry for casual exploration.

Lack of Spatial Continuity: Most web AR experiences are ephemeral—once the browser tab closes, the scene is lost. RoomCraft AR bridges this gap by introducing spatial memory, allowing users to build a persistent virtual room over time.

3. Core Product Pillars

A. Dual Summoning Ecosystem (Catalog & Physical QR Cards)

In-App Catalog: Users can browse a curated library of 3D objects or upload their own 3D models to place in their room.

Physical QR Summoning: Users can scan physical cards, stickers, posters, or packaging. Pointing the camera at a physical QR code immediately unlocks and materializes that specific 3D item directly in front of them in the room.

B. Dynamic & Interactive Object Variety

Static Decor: Clean, beautifully rendered furniture, plants, art frames, and desktop ornaments.

Animated Models: Dynamic kinetic sculptures, idling characters, and animated creatures that bring life and motion into the physical room.

Interactive Items: Functional virtual items that respond to user touch, such as an ambient desk lamp that turns on and off with realistic illumination, or audio-enabled gadgets.

C. Persistent Room Memory (Hybrid Tracking)

Anchor-Based Calibration: Users can place a physical reference anchor (a printed QR card on their desk or wall) that acts as the room's coordinate origin. Scanning this anchor restores all placed items to their exact physical locations across multiple visits.

Markerless Flexibility: Users who prefer not to use a physical marker can freely place items on detected floors or tables for quick, spontaneous sessions.

Multi-Room Support: Users can create and switch between different physical rooms (e.g., "Work Desk Setup", "Bedroom Sanctuary", "Gaming Corner").

D. Natural Manipulation & Intelligent Snapping

Free-Form Control: Intuitive touch gestures allow users to drag items along surfaces, twist to rotate, and pinch to resize.

Surface Snapping: A quick toggle ensures objects snap flush onto detected horizontal surfaces (floors, tables) or vertical surfaces (walls for posters/shelves), eliminating floating artifacts.

Lock-in-Place: Users can lock positioned objects to walk around their customized room without accidentally bumping or shifting items.

E. Realistic Environmental Blending

Virtual items cast soft, realistic contact shadows directly onto physical tabletops and floors.

Materials respond dynamically to real-world lighting conditions, ensuring virtual objects match the brightness and mood of the room.

F. Universal Web Accessibility (Android & iOS Parity)

The entire platform functions smoothly across both Android and iOS mobile web browsers, providing an identical premium user experience on both platforms without needing native app downloads.
